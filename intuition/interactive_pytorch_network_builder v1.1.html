What students should notice: a neural network is just repeated arithmetic. Inputs are numbers. A neuron computes z = Σ(weight × input) + bias, then applies an activation. Hidden layers let the network combine features in new ways. The final neuron turns those learned combinations into a prediction. During training, PyTorch changes these weights and biases to reduce prediction error; here, you are doing that job by hand.
