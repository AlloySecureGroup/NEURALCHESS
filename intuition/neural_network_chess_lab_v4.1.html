Teaching idea: chess.js is the rules engine and the neural network is the evaluator. The rules engine generates only legal moves and detects terminal/draw states. The network receives the full 74-value board/move encoding and scores every legal move. The displayed model-optimal move is the legal move with the largest current neural-network output, with an immediate checkmate always taking priority. This is “optimal under the current editable model,” not a claim of perfect chess play.
